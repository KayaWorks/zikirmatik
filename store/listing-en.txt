App name: Tally Counter - Zikirmatik
Short description: Simple, ad-free tally counter. Set a target, tap, count.
Full description:
A clean, ad-free tally counter for dhikr, prayer beads or counting anything.
• One big button to count
• Targets: 33, 99, 100, 1000 or custom
• Vibration and round count when you hit the target
• −1 to undo a mistaken tap
• Total count kept even after reset
• Light and dark theme
• No internet permission, no ads, no data collection
